Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach a real person.

Contact

Email contact@stormstitchgame.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We reply within two business days.

Playing

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, tap to jump, and swipe down to slide. Grab golden spools to stitch the color back and fill your meter. Hitting a hazard costs a heart and lets the storm creep closer. Reach the torn seam at the end of a stage to sew it shut.

How do I earn stars?

Each stage has three stars: one for finishing, one for the stage's own goal (shown on the stage card), and one for stitching the world fully before you reach the seam.

How do I unlock new monsters?

Open the Trophy Case from the title screen. Each locked monster shows what unlocks it: clearing a stage, collecting thread over time, earning stars, playing Daily Storms, building a long streak, or winning a holiday race. A progress bar shows how close you are.

What are gadgets?

Gadgets are special powers you earn by clearing stages, such as the Time Machine, which rewinds the last 3 seconds. Pick one on the stage card. It charges as you grab spools and pull off close calls. Tap the round button when it glows.

What is the Daily Storm?

One course a day, the same for every player, with a special rule like Fog Day or Mirror Day. Your first run is your score, and you can keep playing for practice. A red dot on the title screen means today's storm is waiting.

How do challenge links work?

After a Daily Storm, tap Challenge to send a link with your score. Your friend opens it on an iPhone with Storm Stitch installed and runs the same course to beat you.

When are the holiday events?

Turkey Trot runs from November 20 to December 1: outrun Gobbler to unlock him. Reindeer Race runs from December 2 to January 4 with Comet. Look for the ribbon on the title screen.

Can I watch the story again?

Yes. Tap Story on the title screen, or Watch the story in Settings.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Tap Settings on the title screen. Each one has its own switch.

Does Storm Stitch work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Play it open or closed; the game fills whichever screen you use.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your device and included in your iPhone backup. Restore from that backup and your progress comes with it. Game Center scores and achievements are tied to your Apple Account.
