How it plays

Short runs. Big finishes.

Swipe, jump, stitch

Swipe between three lanes, tap to jump, swipe down to slide. Each golden spool floods the gray world with color behind you.

The storm is chasing

Hit a hazard and the Shredder rolls closer. Its eyes narrow when it smells a win. Keep stitching and it falls back.

Sew the seam shut

Every stage ends at a torn seam. Reach it and your needle zig-zags it closed in a burst of color. Earn up to three stars.
