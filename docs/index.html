The story

The Paper World is coming apart

A storm called the Shredder tears every page to confetti and drains the color. One small leafy monster finds a way to fix it.

How it plays

Short runs. Big finishes.

  1. 1

    Swipe, jump, slide

    Three lanes, one thumb. Dodge erasers, rulers and tumbling boxes.

  2. 2

    Grab the golden spools

    Every spool floods the gray world with color behind you. Miss too many and the storm closes in.

  3. 3

    Sew the seam shut

    Each stage ends at a torn page. Reach it and your needle stitches it closed. Earn up to three stars.
