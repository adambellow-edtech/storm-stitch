Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Applies to the Storm Stitch app for iPhone and to stormstitchgame.com, published by Adam Bellow.

The short version: Storm Stitch does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Game progress. Stars, best scores, unlocked monsters and gadgets, trophies, Daily Storm scores and settings are saved on your device only. They are included in your normal iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Share images. When you share a result, the game draws the picture on your iPhone. It leaves your device only if you choose where to send it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Daily Storm, Endless and star scores and your trophies are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in achievements. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives your Game Center account details. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the Settings app.

Challenge links

When you tap Challenge after a Daily Storm, the game builds a link that holds the day's number, your score and your Game Center nickname (up to 20 characters), or "A friend" if you are not signed in. The link goes only where you choose to send it. Opening it on stormstitchgame.com passes those values to the app, and nothing is stored.

Children

Storm Stitch is made for players of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no chat, no player profiles and no way to message other players. Sharing uses the standard iPhone share sheet, and Game Center features follow the parental controls set in Screen Time.

Ads and purchases

This version of Storm Stitch shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. If a future version adds optional rewarded ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Purchases are always handled by Apple.

This website

stormstitchgame.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Your choices

You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app, or delete the app to remove all saved progress. The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to request or delete on our side, but we are happy to answer questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
